About

I am a third-year PhD student at KIAA, Peking University, advised by Prof. Linhua Jiang. Prior to this, I received a B.S. degree in Astronomy from Peking University in 2024. My current research focuses on the properties and large-scale environments of active galactic nuclei (AGNs), including the nature of Little Red Dots. I also work on data reduction for the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST), particularly NIRCam grism observations.

I was born in Weifang (潍坊), Shandong, a peaceful city in northern China. In my free time, I enjoy traveling around the world, photographing natural landscapes, watching TV series, and play badminton.

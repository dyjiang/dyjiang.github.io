We investigate the Mpc-scale environments of 70 AGNs at z = 1.1–1.5 using a highly complete sample of 3,419 Paα emitters with accurate spectroscopic redshifts from JWST slitless spectroscopic survey. This dataset allows us to robustly trace the environments and quantify the environmental dependence of AGN properties. We measure the eighth-nearest-neighbor surface density of Paα emitters around each AGN, probing ∼1.5 pMpc scale environments.
